features:
  - title: Zero backend
    details: No server, no account, no telemetry. The extension fetches public provider status feeds directly from your browser.
  - title: Local & private
    details: Settings, rules, and notifications live in chrome.storage. The only requests are to the providers you enable — and, optionally, your own LLM endpoint.
  - title: Multi-cloud
    details: AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Cloudflare, DigitalOcean, Linode, Alibaba Cloud, and Tencent Cloud out of the box. Huawei and Volcano are opt-in experimental.
  - title: Rules that signal
    details: Subscribe to the providers, regions, and severities you care about and get a browser notification only when it matters.
  - title: Optional AI briefings
    details: Bring your own OpenAI-compatible key to generate short incident summaries. The key stays on your machine.
  - title: Bilingual UI
    details: English and 简体中文, auto-detected from your browser language.
